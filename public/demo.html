What Teal does

Every screen below is the app itself.

01

Live T arrivals

The next trains at every stop near you, counting down live, with crowding by car on each trip.

Web and iPhone
02

Bluebikes near you

Classic bikes, e-bikes and open docks at the closest stations, before you walk over.

Web and iPhone
03

Plan any trip

Train, bike, both or walking, fastest first, with the weather for the ride.

Web and iPhone
04

Find a dock, by voice

Say where you’re riding. Teal picks a dock with room and a backup, re-checks every 45 seconds, re-routes if it fills, and reads updates aloud.

iPhone
05

Record runs and rides

Time, distance and pace for runs and Bluebike rides. No account needed.

iPhone
06

Claim your territory

Finish a loop and the ~50 m squares inside it are yours for the month. Works without an account, right on your phone.

iPhone
07

Run with your crew

Weekly leaderboards, friends and their weekly miles. Sign in with Apple or Google to join in.

iPhone

The web demo shows live T, Bluebikes and the planner. The iPhone features run in Expo Go from the open-source project. Leaderboard names in the video and screenshots are sample data.

Teal is an independent project and is not affiliated with the MBTA or Bluebikes. Unlock and pay in the Bluebikes app.
