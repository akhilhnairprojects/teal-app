# STORYLINES — v2 feature breakdown (2026-10-26)

All three are the mix the user asked for: one Boston day as the story, a numbered breakdown of
the eight features riding on it, and one voice narrating both. 45 s, 1080×1080, VO + burned-in
captions + music.

## A — "One day, eight taps" (recommended)

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day · one frame that rebuilds. The phone never leaves
  the centre; the world behind it and a time-of-day chip change around it.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a Boston day from 7:42 AM to 7:10 PM, told in the app's own
  screens. Each tap brings the next feature onto the same phone while a big "01 … 08" counter and
  the sky colour move through the day, so it reads as both a story and a checklist.
- **First two seconds:** "7:42 AM" chip, the phone already showing Park Street's Red Line counting
  down "2 min", the counter landing on "01".
- **Beats:** hook (7:42, the T live) → 02 Bluebikes → 03 planner + weather → 04 "Charles/MGH,"
  spoken, dock found → 05 evening run recording → 06 too-fast warning → 07 loop claims squares →
  08 leaderboard + Apple/Google sign-in → claim → "teal." end card.
- **Look:** Teal's own theme. Dawn mint, noon light, evening deep teal behind the phone; Manrope
  800 numerals; the MBTA line colours only on line pills.
- **Sound:** warm minimal electronic bed that brightens through the day; a soft tick on each
  counter change; the voice like a friend walking you through their day.

Why first: a stranger follows a day they know, and the phone stays big enough to read on a feed.

## B — "The map is the day" (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a flight through one world. The camera travels across the app's own Boston
  map from home to Park Street, to a dock near Kendall, to a loop along the Charles; each stop is
  a numbered pin that opens into that feature's screen.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the whole day as one journey across the city, ending with
  the run's loop filling in territory squares on the same map. The most striking picture, but the
  screens get less time each.
- **First two seconds:** a top-down Boston map, a pin dropping on Park Street with "01" and live
  arrivals unfolding from it.
- **Beats:** hook (pin 01, the T) → 02 → … → 07 the loop fills squares → 08 leaderboard → pull back
  to the whole city → end card.
- **Look:** the app's map style with teal route lines; screens open from pins as cards.
- **Sound:** a travelling, pulsing bed; a whoosh on each flight between pins.

## C — "Eight before nine"

- **Device / form:** type carries the argument · cut on the beat. Huge numerals slam in on the
  music; each number cuts to its screen for a beat, with a small time stamp in the corner.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** fast, punchy and loud, built for X's scroll. Less of a story
  and more of a countdown of what Teal does.
- **First two seconds:** "8 things" filling the frame, "01" cutting in on the downbeat with the T
  countdown.
- **Beats:** title → 01 … 08 on the beat → claim → end card.
- **Look:** flat colour fields from the Teal palette swapping every half beat, giant Manrope
  numerals, screens hard-cut in.
- **Sound:** a driving 120 BPM track; cuts land on the beat; the voice quick and confident.
